## Summary

Logan's Hand-built **Tipping Point** experience for the Aggies Data Hackathon: Next.js **App Router** home with a **dashboard shell** (sidebar + chat composer), **`@/features/tipping-point`** mosaic (portfolio, org detail, IRS revenue chart, People, Org vs peer panels), **API routes** for Gemini and nonprofit filings, and a **reference Vite app** under `app/data-hackathon-dashboard/`.
