# Judging And Deliverables Brief

## What We Are Building

The product answers three questions for funders and advisors:

- Who is financially resilient?
- Which nonprofits are at tipping risk?
- What intervention is most likely to improve the outlook?

The submission should feel like a decision tool, not a data dump.

## Judging Emphasis

- `40%` financial insights
- `60%` solution development, including storytelling and usefulness

That means the model matters, but the demo must clearly show how the output helps a human make a better decision.

## Round-One Deliverables

- Slide deck
- Code
- Five minute video

Keep the deck under about `15` slides unless there is a strong reason to go longer.

## What Judges Need To See

- A crisp problem statement
- A defensible method for identifying resilient and risky nonprofits
- Clear evidence that the rankings are interpretable
- A practical intervention view, not just a score
- A storyline that ties the analytics to a concrete decision

## Narrative Guardrails

- Do not claim causal certainty.
- Do not over-index on model complexity if the output is not explainable.
- Do not present the raw 990 corpus as the product.
- Do present a usable workflow for screening, inspection, and action.

## Recommended Submission Shape

- One-line thesis
- A small set of key metrics or signals
- A portfolio view that ranks organizations
- An organization detail view that explains drivers
- A scenario or intervention view
- A short memo or recommendation output

## Judging Checklist

- The story is easy to follow in five minutes.
- The deck can be understood without the live app.
- The code reproduces the outputs shown in the deck.
- The chosen hero cases support the thesis.
- The intervention advice is specific and grounded in evidence.
