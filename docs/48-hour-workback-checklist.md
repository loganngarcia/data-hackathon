# 48 Hour Workback Checklist

## Principle

Work backward from the final five minute demo. Every checkpoint should produce something a judge could see, not just internal progress.

## First 6 Hours

- Confirm the problem framing and non-negotiables.
- Pull the raw data into the working pipeline.
- Inventory schema, years, and obvious data quality issues.
- Stand up the canonical data contract and artifact naming.
- Draft the slide skeleton and demo narrative early.

## Hours 6 to 18

- Finish normalization and the base analytic panel.
- Establish the first-pass resilience and risk signals.
- Build the first version of portfolio ranking and organization detail outputs.
- Pick provisional hero cases.
- Replace guesses with concrete examples from the data.

## Hours 18 to 30

- Tighten feature engineering and scoring.
- Add explanation output for top drivers.
- Build the scenario or intervention view.
- Start screenshotting the actual product flow for the deck.
- Rewrite the story around the strongest evidence, not the original hypothesis.

## Hours 30 to 40

- Lock the hero cases.
- Draft the deck with near-final visuals.
- Record the five minute script against the live flow.
- Fix anything that is confusing, slow, or visually noisy.

## Hours 40 to 48

- Run end-to-end validation from data to deck.
- Verify the code, video, and slides all say the same thing.
- Trim any slide or feature that does not support the final thesis.
- Leave buffer for rendering, packaging, and a final human read-through.

## Daily Execution Rules

- Keep one person responsible for the narrative.
- Keep one person responsible for data correctness.
- Keep one person responsible for the final demo path.
- Do not let unfinished analysis delay the draft story.
- If a result is not helping the pitch, cut it.
