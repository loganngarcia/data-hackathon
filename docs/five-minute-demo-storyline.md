# Five Minute Demo Storyline

## Core Arc

The demo should move from broad screening to a specific decision:

1. Show the portfolio view and identify the nonprofit landscape.
2. Open one resilient leader and explain why it looks healthy.
3. Open one tipping-risk nonprofit and show the warning signals.
4. Test one intervention and show the expected direction of change.
5. End with a short recommendation or memo that tells the user what to do next.

## Suggested Script Outline

### 0:00 to 0:30

- Frame the problem: nonprofit finances are uneven, and decision-makers need a fast way to find who is resilient, who is vulnerable, and where support matters most.
- State the product thesis: turn messy 990 data into an actionable resilience and intervention workflow.

### 0:30 to 1:30

- Show the portfolio screener.
- Point out that the system ranks organizations by resilience and tipping risk.
- Call out one or two high-signal metrics without drowning in detail.

### 1:30 to 2:30

- Open the resilient hero case.
- Explain the main signals that support the ranking.
- Emphasize that the output is interpretable, not just a black-box score.

### 2:30 to 3:30

- Open the tipping-risk case.
- Show what changed over time and why the nonprofit looks fragile.
- Highlight the specific warning pattern, such as revenue volatility, weak reserves, or concentration risk.

### 3:30 to 4:30

- Run one intervention scenario.
- Show how the score or outlook shifts.
- Make clear that this is a forecasted scenario, not a causal guarantee.

### 4:30 to 5:00

- Close with the memo or recommendation view.
- Summarize the decision value in one sentence.
- Land on the thesis: the tool helps advisors identify resilient nonprofits, catch tipping risk early, and prioritize the most useful intervention.

## Delivery Notes

- Use short sentences.
- Keep transitions explicit.
- Narrate what changed, not just what is visible.
- If anything looks noisy, pivot to the interpretation rather than the technical detail.
